# LinkedIn Ads Research

## When to use

Use this skill for LinkedIn Ad Library research where professional audience,
B2B positioning, lead generation, recruiting, or thought leadership context is
important.

## Tool workflow

1. Search with `search_linkedin_ads` using the brand, competitor, product category, or campaign theme.
2. Apply available country or date filters when market context matters.
3. Group results by advertiser, message angle, CTA, format, and funnel role.
4. Use `ad_reader` on available image or video media before inferring creative strategy.

## Research defaults

- Treat LinkedIn as a professional intent channel; look for proof, credibility, offers, and audience specificity.
- Separate employer-brand ads from product, event, webinar, report, and demo campaigns.
- Compare hooks and CTAs across competitors instead of relying only on volume.

## Output

Return key advertisers, audience assumptions, offer types, CTA patterns,
content formats, funnel role, and 3-5 evidence-backed recommendations for B2B
or professional messaging.
